Client Requests
Search and pick a dish the client insists on. You'll choose which day(s) it must appear on after adding it.
Special Client Request
Paste the client's special instructions below, and/or upload a PDF. Typing a P/F/C target (e.g. "P 100g, F 60g, C 100g") fills the PFC fields and generates the plan automatically (e.g. a custom breakdown like "Breakfast — mixed, 300-350g, avoid jamón/bacon/white rice"). Claude will read it and pull out an avoid-list and notes you can apply to this plan.
Delivery & Schedule
Choose exactly which meal occasions the client receives. The generator will not add food to meal slots outside this schedule. Four meals includes a snack.
Include a protein shake with breakfast (only when Breakfast is selected)

Enter the number of delivery days requested by the client (1–60). This controls the meal-plan length and the total delivery-fee calculation.
Fixed rule: food cost must not exceed 30% of the discounted menu price. The maximum discount is calculated from the selected plan's actual menu price and food cost; it cannot be manually increased.
Food cost calculation (internal — not printed)
"Show only meal plan" hides every price, the delivery fee and the grand total, on screen, in print/PDF and in the CSV — use it when the client shouldn't see prices. You can switch it after generating.
Open an Existing Meal Plan PDF
Choose a PDF made with this app (Print / Save PDF). It is read into the editor, so you can change it — Reshuffle a day, switch the price / discount display, print it again — without generating a new plan. Needs an internet connection the first time, to load the PDF reader.
